## Participant information and consent — tutoring training materials pilot

**Who is running this?** [Program owner name] — [contact email]. The materials being tested are open-source tutor training modules published at https://iwyusdaz.github.io/tutor-training-course/.

**What am I being asked to do?** Take part in one session of about 60–75 minutes. You will: answer three short written scenarios as if responding to a student; read two self-paced training modules (about 20 minutes each); complete a short in-browser practice activity; answer three more written scenarios; and take part in a short interview about your experience.

**Why?** To find out what is confusing, unusable, or unhelpful in the materials — so they can be improved. This is a usability and clarity pilot. **It is not a test of you**; there are no right or wrong participants, and "this doesn't work" is exactly the data being sought.

**Do I have to?** No. Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question, stop at any point, or withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without any consequence. You may ask for your data to be deleted at any time before the report is finalized (the owner will tell you the date).

**What data is collected?**
- Your written scenario responses and practice activity choices (labeled with a participant code, e.g., P3 — not your name).
- Session notes about where you got stuck or confused.
- An optional first name or initials, only if you agree to be quoted.
- **No** demographic data, contact details (beyond a way to send your thank-you, if you choose to leave one), recordings of your screen, or recordings of you, unless you separately agree to audio notes during the interview.

**How will my data be used?** Anonymized responses and (with permission) short quotes will be included in a pilot report published on the project website. Raw sheets are stored in a single folder, and deleted after the report is finalized. Data is never sold or shared beyond what this page says.

**What will be published?** Aggregate, descriptive results ("4 of 5 participants found X confusing"), and quotes only if you tick the box below. Your name will not appear unless you choose to be named — and no one is required to be named.

**Conflicts and honesty notes.** The materials were produced with AI assistance; the report discloses this. The report labels who did what: participant (you), scorers (humans, blinded), and any AI-assisted analysis. No claims about student learning are made or will be made from this pilot.

**Questions?** Ask now or any time at [contact email]. You keep a copy of this form.

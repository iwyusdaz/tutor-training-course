# Live practice sessions

Two facilitated 60-minute rehearsals that new tutors complete in weeks 1–2 of onboarding ([timeline](../program/onboarding-timeline.md)). Self-paced modules teach the moves; these sessions rehearse them out loud with feedback — interpersonal skill does not transfer from reading.

| Session | When | Rehearses | Transcripts used |
|---|---|---|---|
| [A · The session routine](session-a.md) | Week 1, after Modules 1–2 | The five-block 30-minute routine, end to end, three times | [T1](../transcripts/t1.md) (model), [T5](../transcripts/t5.md) (anti-model) |
| [B · Handling errors](session-b.md) | Week 2, after Modules 3–4 | Probing questions, wait time, the hint ladder, error responses | [T2](../transcripts/t2.md) (model), [T6](../transcripts/t6.md) (anti-model) |
| [Role-play cards](role-play-cards.md) | Both sessions | Student/tutor/observer roles, per round | — |

Both guides include an online-delivery variant (breakout rooms, captions-on policy, materials in advance) and an observer checklist keyed to the [observation rubric](../coaching/observation-rubric.md) — so the first practice observations double as calibration reps for coordinators.

{: .note }
**Fictional program, real practice design.** Bridge Math Tutoring's sessions, students and tutors on these pages are fictional. Role-play students are adults playing middle schoolers; the "misconceptions" they simulate come from the same research base as the [exit-ticket bank](../tools/exit-ticket-bank.md).

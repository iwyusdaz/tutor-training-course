# Self-paced modules

Five modules, one per [competency domain](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/tutor-training-course/blob/main/research/competency-model.md), each designed for **25 minutes or less**: a scenario hook, the key idea, a transcript model, practice with feedback, a "try this in your next session" action, and a reflection prompt. Objectives are observable and each links to the rubric row and fidelity metric it supports ([full alignment table](../program/alignment-table.md)).

| # | Module | Domain | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Your role and relationships](modules/1-your-role-and-relationships/) | Relationships | ~20 |
| 2 | [The 30-minute session](modules/2-the-30-minute-session/) | Session structure | ~25 |
| 3 | [Questions, wait time and errors](modules/3-questions-wait-time-and-errors/) | Questioning and errors | ~25 |
| 4 | [Feedback and motivation](modules/4-feedback-and-motivation/) | Feedback and motivation | ~20 |
| 5 | [Using exit tickets and data](modules/5-using-exit-tickets-and-data/) | Data use | ~25 |
| — | [Safeguarding (placeholder)](safeguarding.md) | — | per your organization |

{: .warning }
Complete your **organization's own safeguarding training** before any contact with students — see the [placeholder page](safeguarding.md). This program does not teach safeguarding law.

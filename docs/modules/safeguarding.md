# Safeguarding · complete your organization's training

{: .label .label-red }
Required before any contact with students · Not part of this program

## This is a placeholder, on purpose

Child protection, mandated reporting, digital-communication rules and student-privacy obligations **vary by jurisdiction and by organization**, and they change. A generic module written into an open training program would be wrong somewhere, and wrong safeguarding guidance is worse than none.

**Therefore:** before your first session — before you meet students, before you observe, before any tutoring — complete the safeguarding training your own organization requires. Your site coordinator confirms it is done and recorded.

## What your organization's training will cover (orientation only — your training is authoritative)

Typical topics in US K-12 partner organizations:

- Recognizing and reporting suspected abuse or neglect (mandated-reporter rules differ by state)
- Approved communication channels with students — almost always organization-managed tools only, never personal phone numbers, personal social media or private messaging
- Physical-space rules: doors, visibility, one-on-one settings
- What you must hand to staff and never handle yourself (disclosures, suspicions, conflicts)
- Student data and privacy rules — including the initials-only rule this program's [tracker](../tools/student-progress-tracker.md) already follows

## One line in every session

When in doubt about anything involving a student's safety or privacy: **stop, and tell your site coordinator the same day.** The escalation habit in [Module 5](5-using-exit-tickets-and-data.md) is the coaching loop; this is the safety loop — and it always outranks it.

# Pilot report

{: .label .label-red }
**Status: PENDING — the pilot has not been run.** This page is a prepared template with the protocol, tasks and scoring design already fixed ([protocol](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/tutor-training-course/blob/main/evaluation/human-protocol/pilot-protocol.md)). When the pilot runs, the report is written into this page and the status label changes. **No results, quotes, or numbers appear on this page, and none are claimed anywhere in this repo.**

## Planned design (fixed in advance — registered before data)

**Participants:** 3–5 adults who have tutored or intend to (per the [protocol](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/tutor-training-course/blob/main/evaluation/human-protocol/pilot-protocol.md): consent, no personal data collected beyond optional first name, no minors).

**Materials under test:** [Module 3](../modules/3-questions-wait-time-and-errors.md), [Module 4](../modules/4-feedback-and-motivation.md), and the [spot-the-move practice](../transcripts/practice/index.html) — the smallest slice that exercises the program's hardest skill.

**Flow (60–75 min per participant):**
1. **Pre-task** — respond in writing to three scripted student errors ([error set A](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/tutor-training-course/blob/main/evaluation/human-protocol/pilot-protocol.md#task-materials)).
2. **Study** — Modules 3 and 4 at their own pace, then the practice tool.
3. **Post-task** — three *new* scripted errors (set B, parallel difficulty).
4. **Survey** — clarity, usefulness, the "one thing I'd change" question.

**Scoring:** the written pre/post responses are scored with the [rubric's Domain 3 anchors](../coaching/observation-rubric.md#domain-3--questioning-and-errors) (probing question · expose-don't-correct · wait-time respect in writing · no takeover), **blind to pre/post ordering** (sheets are shuffled and de-identified before scoring — the [blind scoring sheet](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/tutor-training-course/blob/main/evaluation/human-protocol/blind-scoring-sheet.md) implements this).

## What the results will be allowed to claim — and not

| Will say | Will NOT say |
|---|---|
| "Descriptive comparison of written responses, n=3–5, per the rubric anchors" | Any learning-gain or significance claim |
| "Participants found X confusing; revised Y" | "The training works" or "validated with users" |
| Quoted feedback (with consent), labeled as pilot-participant quotes | Anything generalizable to real tutors or students |
| ≥3 revisions made in response, logged in the [changelog](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/tutor-training-course/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) | That the revisions were 'proven' by the pilot |

Small n, no control, self-selected adults: the pilot's job is to find *clarity and usability failures* in the materials — ambiguous instructions, unrealistic scenarios, missing feedback — before real tutors meet them.

## Report skeleton (to be filled after the pilot)

1. **Dates, participants (n, background), consent status** — table
2. **Pre/post rubric scores** — per participant per anchor, descriptive; no averages presented as gains
3. **Participant quotes** — with consent, labeled
4. **What confused people** — task-level findings
5. **Revisions made** — linked diffs / changelog entries (minimum 3 to claim the pilot was used)
6. **Limitations** — n, self-selection, writing-vs-live gap (written responses can't show wait time), no student data, authors scored with the AI-judge method labeled as such

# Bridge Tutor Academy

A blended training program that prepares new tutors — college students and community members, most with no teaching background — to deliver **high-impact math tutoring with consistency**: the same session routine, the same student-facing moves, every session, so that quality survives scale.

Bridge Math Tutoring is a **fictional organization**: a nonprofit partnering with six middle schools, whose paid part-time tutors deliver in-school sessions to groups of 2–4 students in Grade 7–8 linear equations, 3 × 30 minutes per week. All transcripts, dashboards and program data on this site are fictional or simulated and labeled as such.

{: .note }
**Why this program exists:** small tutoring studies show large learning gains, but large programs deliver roughly a third to a half as much ([Kraft, Schueler & Falken 2024](https://edworkingpapers.com/ai24-1031)) — and what erodes at scale is implementation: dosage, consistency, and tutor skill. Training, coaching and fidelity measurement are the countermeasures. The [research notes](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/tutor-training-course/blob/main/research/evidence-notes.md) trace every claim to a primary source.

## The competency model underneath it all

Everything on this site trains, observes or measures the same five domains, defined once in the [competency model](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/tutor-training-course/blob/main/research/competency-model.md):

1. **Relationships** — greet by name, reference last session, spread attention
2. **Session structure** — the five-part 30-minute routine, exit ticket every session
3. **Questioning and errors** — probing question, hint ladder (levels 0–5), wait time, worked example after repeated failure
4. **Feedback and motivation** — specific feedback, confirm and ask why, offer a similar problem, normalize mistakes
5. **Data use** — give, record and read the exit ticket; adjust the next plan

The move names and the hint ladder are shared with the companion project **[Socratic AI Tutor](https://iwyusdaz.github.io/socratic-ai-tutor/)** — the same moves, encoded for an AI.

## The four-week onboarding timeline

This is the schedule a new tutor follows inside the fictional program ([full version with facilitator notes](program/onboarding-timeline.md)):

| Week | Self-paced | Live practice | With students |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Modules 1–2 · safeguarding training | Live Session A: role-play the routine | Observing a veteran tutor |
| 2 | Modules 3–4 · transcript practice | Live Session B: role-play error handling | Co-tutoring (second adult in the room) |
| 3 | Module 5 | — | First solo sessions · observation #1 + debrief |
| 4+ | — | Monthly community of practice | Coaching cycle every 2–3 weeks · observation #2 and #3 |

{: .warning }
**Safeguarding:** child protection, mandated reporting and digital-communication rules vary by jurisdiction and organization. This program does **not** teach safeguarding law. The [safeguarding placeholder](modules/safeguarding.md) directs tutors to complete their organization's own required training before any contact with students.

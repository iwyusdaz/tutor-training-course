# Onboarding timeline (the fictional program's)

{: .label .label-purple }
This is **course content** — the schedule a new Bridge Math Tutoring tutor follows in their first four weeks. It is not the development schedule of this website.

## The four weeks

| Week | Self-paced | Live rehearsal | With students | Coaching |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | [Module 1](../modules/1-your-role-and-relationships.md) + [Module 2](../modules/2-the-30-minute-session.md) (~45 min) · **organization's safeguarding training** ([placeholder](../modules/safeguarding.md)) · meet your coordinator | [Session A](../live-practice/session-a.md): role-play the session routine (60 min) | Observe one veteran session (30 min, with the [rubric print sheet](../coaching/observation-rubric.md) — as an observer, you learn the moves' names) | Welcome check-in (15 min): your group assignment, your school's logistics |
| **2** | [Module 3](../modules/3-questions-wait-time-and-errors.md) + [Module 4](../modules/4-feedback-and-motivation.md) (~45 min) · [spot-the-move practice](../transcripts/practice/index.html) (20 min) · [transcripts T1](../transcripts/t1.md) + [T5](../transcripts/t5.md) | [Session B](../live-practice/session-b.md): role-play error handling (60 min) | **Co-tutor** two sessions (you lead warm-up + exit ticket; veteran leads the rest) | Co-tutor debrief (15 min) |
| **3** | [Module 5](../modules/5-using-exit-tickets-and-data.md) (~25 min) · first solo [session plans](../tools/session-plan-template.md) reviewed by coordinator | — | **First solo sessions** (3 × 30 min: Mon/Wed/Thu) | **Observation #1** (30 min) + [debrief within 48 h](../coaching/coaching-conversation-guide.md): one strength, one focus move |
| **4** | Focus move practice from observation #1 ([self-reflection](../tools/tutor-self-reflection.md) weekly) | Monthly community of practice begins (theme: the month's rubric patterns, anonymized) | Full solo schedule (3/week) · [tracker](../tools/student-progress-tracker.md) discipline | **Observation #2** + debrief · **first [coaching cycle](../coaching/coaching-cycle.md) re-check** |

**Week 5 and beyond:** solo schedule continues; [coaching cycle](../coaching/coaching-cycle.md) every 2–3 weeks; **observation #3 in week 9** (the growth measurement window: rubric 1 → 3, target +0.5 — [measurement plan](measurement-plan.md)); monthly community of practice; safeguarding refresher per your organization's policy.

## Design notes (why the weeks are shaped this way)

- **Rehearsal before students.** Both live sessions precede solo work — the [role-play cards](../live-practice/role-play-cards.md) carry scripted misconceptions so the first time a real student says "just tell me," it's the tutor's fifth time hearing it, not their first.
- **Observe before tutoring.** Week 1's observation with the rubric in hand teaches the vocabulary; tutors score a veteran's session *low-stakes* before anyone scores theirs.
- **The data loop starts week 3.** Module 5 lands exactly when tutors start solo sessions, so the tracker habit forms with the sessions rather than being retrofitted.
- **One focus move at a time, from the start.** Observation #1's debrief concentrates on a single move (the [coaching cycle](../coaching/coaching-cycle.md) rule), and the community of practice reviews themes — not individuals — monthly.
- **Consistency is designed, not hoped.** Every student meets the same tutor from their first session (≥85% same-tutor target is set by [scheduling], not by luck), because the dosage research the program is built on names continuity as a condition of impact — [evidence notes §2](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/tutor-training-course/blob/main/research/evidence-notes.md).

{: .note }
**Fictional program.** Bridge Math Tutoring does not exist; the timeline demonstrates blended onboarding design — self-paced content, spaced rehearsal, early real practice with close support, and measurement from day one.
